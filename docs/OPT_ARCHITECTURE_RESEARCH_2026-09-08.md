## 0. The unifying law discovered by the puzzle (the meta-result)

R2-F1 (dirty-set incremental layout recomputation, arXiv 2411.10659v3)
measured: recompute-only-dirty beats full recompute **3.23× mean, ≥5.85×
when <1% of fields are dirty (60.7% of cases), 10–100× outliers**.

This is the theoretical backbone of our entire >100x guard class: our
area-map (1,945x–170,612x), lifecycle (>571x) and boot-scan (10x–100x)
wins are exactly the <1%-dirty regime. The puzzle assembles into a
**principled candidate-mining pipeline**:

> **Guard-candidate detector = mutation:query ratio census per surface.**
> Surfaces with query-rate >> mutation-rate are the >100x class. Surfaces
> with comparable rates are honest 1.1-2x class (fluid-push: measured
> machinery 23-40%, verdict 1.4-1.6x — consistent). No census currently
> measures dirty-rates systematically — boot-scan auto-detection of
> high-query/low-mutation surfaces is the pipeline's missing tool
> (candidate: TASK-84 shape).

## 1. Puzzle 1 — dfc-on-Paper: density-function DAG → JVM bytecode

* Pieces: (a) R1: C2ME compiles vanilla density-function DAGs into JVM
  bytecode ("dfc"), +30% published on datapack-heavy worldgen — no JNI;
  (b) TASK-82 census: 6.5% of worker CPU is ArrayList$Itr.next +
  checkForComodification inside `NoiseChunk.fillAllDirectly` interpreter
  walk (measured on OUR box); (c) R3#7: straight-line generated bytecode
  JITs clean (no iterator dispatch, no megamorphic DAG walk).
* Assembly: weave-side dfc port for Paper/Purpur (Fabric module today —
  the port gap is R1's finding #9c). Identical outputs (it is a compiler,
  not a reimplementation). No JNI (consistent with our noise-kernel
  refutations: the win is machinery removal, not native math).
* Honest sizing: ~1.05–1.10x on vanilla-gen worker wall (6.5% machinery);
  datapack-heavy servers would see more (C2ME's +30%). **CANDIDATE** —
  only worth building if a datapack-heavy profile is in scope; vanilla
  gen alone does not justify it.
* Verdict rationale vs TASK-79: NOT refuted — TASK-79 shut the NATIVE
  noise-kernel channel; dfc is bytecode-compilation, a different mechanism.

## 6. Puzzle 6 — formalized JIT heuristics for the weaving pipeline (GO)

* Pieces: R3#7: published HotSpot constants — `HugeMethodLimit=8000`
  (C2 refuses bigger methods), `MaxInlineSize=35`, `FreqInlineSize=325`,
  megamorphic past `TypeProfileWidth=2` → no devirtualization/inlining;
  (b) our TASK-74 G-AB discovery (11KB barrier body → native swap 5-8x)
  was an instance of the >8000 rule; our inlining-barrier win matched.
* Assembly: encode into the agent's audit/heuristics: body >8000 opcodes
  behind a hot callsite = native-swap candidate; callsite with >2
  receivers = guard/direct-dispatch candidate. **GO — zero-risk
  documentation + tooling work.**

## 7. Puzzle 7 — cheap A/Bs (CANDIDATE queue)

* **Graal JIT** (R3#2): Oracle GraalVM for JDK 21, -XX:+UseJVMCICompiler:
  +23% geomean vs C2 (1,112 JMH runs, x86_64), stronger on megamorphic
  dispatch — exactly our census profile (megamorphic DoubleList walks).
  A/B = swap JDK, dormant boot + P500, verify re-JIT of redefined
  classes. Cheap, reversible, no code changes.
* **JVM flags** (R3#8): `-XX:+UseStringDeduplication` (13.5% of live heap
  is duplicate strings — JEP 192) on our 2GB heap; `CICompilerCount` for
  boot compile stalls. Cheap A/B pass.
* **Vector API** (R3#6): up to 16x on batch float kernels INSIDE the JIT
  (no JNI floor) — the correct tool where native noise kernels lost;
  incubator status on JDK 21 = documented risk. CANDIDATE for a single
  batched-noise spike IF worldgen profiling ever shows a batch-shaped
  kernel need (our census shows interpreter machinery, not math — so
  priority LOW).
