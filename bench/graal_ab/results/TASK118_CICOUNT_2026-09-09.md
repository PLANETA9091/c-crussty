## Interpretation

CICompilerCount=3 on 2 vCPU buys nothing measurable: boot compilation is not
the boot-wall bottleneck on this box, and the burst-cpu hint dissolves under
position control. The 2-thread default (1 C1 + 1 JVMCI) is already matched
to the 2-vCPU envelope; adding a compiler thread only adds RSS. Together
with phase 1 (dedup NULL), TASK-118 closes both OPT_ARCH §7 R3#8 flag
candidates: the operator-of-record config survives flag-level scrutiny
unchanged, consistent with TASK-116's isolation finding that the win is the
JIT itself, not its tuning knobs.
