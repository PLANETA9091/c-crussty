# PAPER_AB_2026-10-03 — vanilla Paper vs Paper+c-crussty (real-server A/B)

Paired A/B on a real kernel (Purpur 1.21.10-2535-HEAD, Minecraft 1.21.10,
OpenJDK 21.0.12.1, Debian sandbox, 2 CPUs, 4 GB RAM). Protocol derived from
the canon e2e harnesses (`bench/e2e/run_worldgen_ab.sh` TASK-63 /
`run_perlin_ab.sh` TASK-74 protocol v2). Harness:
`bench/ab/run_paper_ab.sh` (+ `rcon.py`, `env_full.sh`,
`aggregate_paper_ab.py`). Raw: `paper_ab_raw.tsv` (17 runs), per-run boot
logs + forceload query receipts in this directory.

## Arms

| arm | what runs | legs |
|---|---|---:|
| **A** | vanilla Paper: plain `java -Xms512M -Xmx1536m -jar purpur-1.21.10.jar --nogui` | 7 |
| **B** | Paper + c-crussty DEFAULT posture: CRUSSTY runtime agent → module, factory lever gates (perlin bridge armed+self-tested, area-map patched+self-tested, improved_noise dormant, everything else dormant) | 6 |
| **F** | Paper + c-crussty FULL surface (owner mandate): every env-gated lever ON simultaneously (improved_noise, noise_fill, fluid guard/bitmask/dirty/free, paletted, alloc_diet, inside cache/epoch/batch/bitmask, flush_diet, zero_cursor, zero_alloc, flat_traversal, region_threads=2 chain, batch_collector, bu_defer, travel_diet, skip_store_bb, selector bulk, poi guard, batch API=on). lever_flag STRICT lanes stay dormant (mutually exclusive by design) | 4 |

Identical in all arms: world (fixed seed 3053459, byte-identical restore from
`world_ab_seed.tar.gz` before every run), JVM flags, server.properties
(RCON enabled as the command channel in both arms), burst workload, worldgen
state. The only differential is the agent + its env gates.

## Burst workload

`forceload add 1600 1600 1727 1727` + `forceload add -1728 -1728 -1601 -1601`
(TASK-63 canon coords: two 8x8-chunk squares at |x,z| = 1600..1727 — 128
chunks of fresh worldgen per run). Completion = symmetric idle gate (child
CPU rate < 0.15 cores for 3 consecutive 0.5 s samples after a > 0.5 cores
active phase), hard cap 420 s.

## Results (medians, raw in `paper_ab_raw.tsv`)

| metric | A (vanilla) | B (default) | Δ B vs A | F (full) | Δ F vs A |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| cpu_burst (CPU-s) | 37.91 | 35.66 | **−5.9%** | 57.27 | **+51.1%** |
| t_burst (wall s) | 41.0 | 38.0 | **−7.3%** | 55.5 | **+35.4%** |
| boot_s | 15.99 | 15.24 | −4.7% | 16.40 | +2.6% |
| RSS at burst end (kB) | 1,149,212 | 1,163,580 | +1.2% | 1,455,084 | **+26.6%** |

Exact permutation Mann-Whitney (two-sided, full enumeration):

- **A vs B (cpu_burst): p = 0.366; (t_burst): p = 0.121** — directionally
  better (both medians below vanilla) but NOT individually significant at
  n=6/7 on this rig. Consistent with the significant live-server result of
  the same lever family (TASK-74: cpu_burst −11.1%, p = 0.0079, n=5/arm,
  owner rig, larger burst): the sandbox burst is small (128 chunks) and the
  noise lever is a minority share of it.
- **A vs F (cpu_burst): p = 0.0061; (t_burst): p = 0.0242** — the FULL
  surface is significantly SLOWER on a fresh-worldgen burst, with full rank
  separation on cpu (F min 43.29 > A max 39.92). Attribution: the
  architecture/experimental lanes (region_threads, zero_alloc,
  flat_traversal, zero_cursor, inside_* experiments, alloc_diet) spend
  their budget on entity-tick and steady-state lanes, not on chunk-gen, and
  several are FAIL-zone documented in `docs/KERNEL_POLICY.md`. The F arm is
  the measured confirmation that kernel policy = production posture must
  stay default/dormant except proven wins (perlin bridge + area-map).
- RSS: B parity with A (+1.2%); F +26.6% (region-thread scratch buffers +
  memoization caches). Historical pure-inject idle RSS result (−29% vs
  flag-era) measured the idle channel, not the burst-working-set channel —
  both stand, they measure different things.

## Arm validation

Every B/F run printed `[crussty-plugin] native surface live: 98 bridge
classes, 283 natives registered` + `nativeCheck() = 1` live proof; B runs
additionally show `area_map: patched (5075 -> 3320 bytes)`, `area_map
self-test OK (64 rects)`, `perlin_noise: hook armed, retransform rc=0`,
`perlin_noise self-test passed`; F runs show the full lever set armed
(region_threads patches, noise_fill patch computed, inside_cache,
fluid_guard, fluid_bitmask, alloc_diet, improved_noise). F validation gates
in the harness FAIL loudly if improved_noise is still dormant.

## Deviations from canon (documented)

1. Command channel: RCON (localhost, both arms) instead of the stdin-fifo
   relay — sandbox-detached-process constraint; measurement channel is
   symmetric and touches no gameplay surface.
2. Heap: `-Xms512M -Xmx1536m` (2-CPU/4GB sandbox) instead of the historical
   `-Xms512M -Xmx2G`.
3. Burst coords are the canon TASK-63 coordinates; the burst is smaller than
   the owner-rig MineShield-3 forceload (no 6.7 GB world in this sandbox).
4. Interleaving: arms alternate in blocks (A1 B1 B2 A7 F1 F2 A2 F3 A3 F4 A4
   B3 B4 B5 A5 B6 A6) — not strict ABBA; no BENCH.lock holder (single
   session owns the box).
5. CPU-band pairing gate not applied (single-rig series).

